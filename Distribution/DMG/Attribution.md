# DMG artwork attribution

`Music-slur.svg` is **Musical Slur.svg** by 京市 from Wikimedia Commons:
<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Musical_Slur.svg>.

The asset is available under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0
Unported license. The downloaded original is included as
`Music-slur-original.svg`. The DMG artwork adapts its engraved contour into a
filled Bézier slur that is thicker through the middle and thinner at the tips.
The curved ends join tangentially to the slur edges, with no flat cuts or
separate circles. The finished slur sits above the app icons and contains no
notes or staff lines.

The original SVG and its license terms are available at the source page above.
